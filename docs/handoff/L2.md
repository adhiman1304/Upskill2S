# L2 handoff: logo retry (round 2)

Status: done. `npm --prefix client run build` passes. `npm --prefix client run lint` reports no problems in L2's files; the one remaining warning is in `pages/legacy/LibraryPage.tsx`, which another workstream owns. No dev servers were started or restarted.

## Feedback being answered

Round 1 (a rising u with round caps on a radius-6 tile) was "a little round and low definition". It did not look professional or match the plain UI. The owner asked for something sharper. Mid-round the owner added a second requirement: the mark should also work as the "u" of the wordmark.

## Directions explored

All directions are drawn in `client/src/components/brand/explorations.tsx`. In development, open `http://localhost:5174/?brand=1`. Each direction is shown at 16, 28 and 64 px, then inverted, then as a bare glyph in the accent. Screenshot: `docs/screenshots/brand2-explorations.png`.

| | Direction | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| E | **Rising bracket u**: a square-cornered u whose right stem rises one module above the left | **Chosen** |
| E2 | The same u, with the top of the rising stem cut at 45° | More directional, but that diagonal is the only soft edge in the mark, and it reads as a pencil |
| A | Three ascending bars | Crisp at every size, but it is the signal-strength icon and a generic analytics glyph |
| B | Stepped checkmark (level run, 90° drop, 45° climb) | The long diagonal cannot sit on the pixel grid, and at 16 px it reads as a todo-app tick |
| C | Two nested chevrons | Reads as a military rank, an upvote or a "scroll to top" button |
| D | Square split by a diagonal channel | The channel blurs at 16 px, and the shape reads as a slash or a "no" sign |

The gallery also keeps the round 1 mark as a reference row, labelled "Round 1, rejected: too round".

## Why E

- **Sharp by construction.** It is one filled polygon with eight straight edges and no diagonals or curves. Every edge is a multiple of 3 units, so the shape renders with zero anti-aliased pixels at 16, 24 and 32 px on 1x and 2x screens. I measured this by rasterising in headless Chrome; see `brand2-16px-zoom.png`. The same holds at 28 and 36 px on 2x screens. `crispEdges` and `geometricPrecision` gave identical pixels, because no edge is fractional.
- **It owns the name.** It is a letter, not an icon, and it is the only direction that can stand in for the u of "upskill". That made it the only option once the owner added the logotype requirement.
- **Meaning.** One stem rising above the other is the single path upward. It keeps the idea from round 1, now drawn in straight lines.
- **Restraint.** It uses one shape, one color and the same weight throughout. It sits with DM Sans and hairline dividers the way the Linear or Vercel marks sit with their UIs.

## Construction

### Tiled mark (favicon, compact, `LogoMark`)

The mark is drawn on a 24-unit grid with a 3-unit module, so it is an 8 × 8 module grid. The source is `geometry.ts` (`MARK_GLYPH_PATHS`, `MARK_TILE_RADIUS`).

- **Tile:** 24 × 24, corner radius 3 (it was 6 in round 1).
- **Glyph path:** `M6 9H9V15H15V6H18V18H6Z`
  - The glyph box runs from x 6 to 18 and y 6 to 18. It is centred, with 6-unit margins.
  - The left stem runs from x 6 to 9 and y 9 to 18. The right stem runs from x 15 to 18 and y 6 to 18, so it rises one module above the left stem.
  - The bowl runs from y 15 to 18. The counter is a 6 × 6 square.
- **Weight:** 3 units everywhere. That is 2 px at 16 px and 4 px at 32 px.
- **Rendering:** the glyph is drawn with `shape-rendering: crispEdges`. The tile keeps the default rendering so its corners stay smooth.
- **Colors:** in the `accent` tone (the default), a green `#3b5a2a` tile with a white glyph. `inverse` is a white tile with a green glyph. `glyph` is the glyph alone in `currentColor`.

### Logotype (the full `Brand`)

The bracket u is drawn in the text color with no tile, followed by "pskill" in DM Sans 600 at -0.02 em. The glyph is sized in em from DM Sans 600's own "u", which I measured from the font at 400 px. The source is `WORDMARK_U`, in units of 1/1000 em.

- **Width and side bearings:** the advance is 604 and the ink runs from x 60 to 538. Both match the font's u, so the word is exactly as wide as plain "upskill" (measured as 82.9 px at 28 px in both cases).
- **Stroke weight:** stems are 120 wide and the bowl is 100 thick. These match the font's stem width and its thinner bottom stroke, so the stroke contrast matches.
- **Heights:**
  - The left stem runs from the x-height (505) down to the baseline.
  - The right stem rises to 673. That is a third of the x-height above it, the same 3 : 4 ratio as the tiled mark.
  - 673 is below the 720 ascender of k and l, so the letter reads as u and not d.
- **Path:** `M60 168H180V573H418V0H538V673H60Z` in a `0 0 604 673` viewBox.
- **Placement:** the SVG is `0.604em × 0.673em` with `vertical-align: baseline`, so it scales with font size and sits on the baseline. A -0.02 em right margin applies the tracking the u would have had.
- **Alignment check at 28 px:** the bracket's baseline matches the font's u to the device pixel. Its x-height is within 1 device pixel (0.5 CSS px) of the font's u. See `brand2-lockup-zoom.png`.
- **Dot on the i:** the green dot from round 1 is gone. A round accent dot fought the square letter, and the mark carries the idea alone.

## Component API (unchanged signatures)

```tsx
<LogoMark size? tone?="accent"|"inverse"|"glyph" className? title? />  // tiled bracket u
<Wordmark className? text? />    // NEW optional `text`: plain "upskill" fallback
<Brand size?="sm"|"md"|"lg" compact? className? />
```

- `Brand` (full) now renders the logotype only: the bracket u plus "pskill", with no separate tile. `compact` renders the green tiled mark, labelled "Upskill".
- Logotype sizes are `sm` 18 px, `md` 24 px (the default, used in the sidebar) and `lg` 32 px. The sign-in and setup screens get `lg` through context CSS. The mobile top bar is 20 px. Compact mark sizes are unchanged at 22, 28 and 36 px.
- `index.ts` also exports `MARK_TILE_RADIUS` and `WORDMARK_U`. `MARK_GLYPH_PATHS` is still exported, but its paths are now filled shapes rather than strokes.
